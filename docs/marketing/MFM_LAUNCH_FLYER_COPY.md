## Flyer bullet points (use 6–8 on print)

### Community & identity
1. **Your fitness tribe, one place** — connect with athletes and clubs across Mumbai who actually move.
2. **Real people. Real sessions.** — no noise, just workouts, events, and momentum from your city.

### Events
3. **Discover events near you** — runs, yoga, HIIT, cycling, club sessions & more on one map.
4. **RSVP in one tap** — lock in your spot and roll deep with your crew before you arrive.
5. **Host. Fill. Grow.** — clubs and organisers can launch sessions and build a loyal community.

### Social feed
6. **Follow athletes & clubs** — see workouts, wins, and moments from people you respect.
7. **Post your grind** — share photos, updates, and the sessions that keep you consistent.

### Messaging
8. **Stay locked in** — DMs, group chats & event threads so your training circle never goes quiet.

### Discovery
9. **Search the city** — find people, clubs, and events on an interactive map built for Mumbai.

### Profiles
10. **Athlete or club** — your profile is your fitness identity. Followers, events, posts — all in one home.
